The referee's verdict

◈ Its strengths

  • Zero friction.A single HTML file: nothing to install, nothing to configure, you start in one click. No competitor does that.
  • The narrative.The Sensei/Kaï storyline, the kanji drop caps, “the terminal drives the world” — an experience, not a utility.
  • The design.A strong washi identity where the others are austere. This one makes you want to stay.
  • The visualization.The explorer that shows the effect of each command live — ideal for a beginner.

◈ Its limits

  • Simulated terminal, not real.At KodeKloud, Sadservers or OverTheWire, you get a real machine: you can break everything, test everything. The Dojo only knows the commands it anticipates.
  • Pattern-based validation.The checks verify a pattern, not a true system state — a clever student can work around it.
  • No cloud accounts.Progress is local, per device: no sync, no certificate, no leaderboard.
  • Depth.Each topic is a fine introduction; specialists go further in their niche.
