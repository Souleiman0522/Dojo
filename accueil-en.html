The difference

The terminal doesn't leave you in the dark

A cryptic output? The Dojo decodes it right beside, in plain English.

kai@dojo : ~/mission
kai@dojo:~/mission$ ls -l mission.sh
-rwxr-xr-- 1 kai dojo 2048 Aug 3 mission.sh
🧭 The Dojo translates
  • -A file (a folder would show d)
  • rwxYou: read, write, execute7
  • r-xYour group: read and execute5
  • r--Others: read only4
r=4 · w=2 · x=1= 754
👉 chmod 754 mission.sh applies exactly these permissions.

Who we are

Linux enthusiasts, obsessed with teaching

Our obsession: to simplify as much as possible notions reputed to be difficult — to make them clear, concrete and accessible to everyone, from the total beginner to the one who wants to host their own services.

Too many people give up facing a black screen and dry docs. So we built the opposite: a place that is beautiful, narrative and supportive, where every term is explained, every command is told as a story, and where the terminal itself translates what is cryptic.

And we don't stop at theory: once the basics are learned, you build for real — your cloud, your media server, your VPN — services you keep and use. Learning Linux like a martial art: through practice, tier after tier.
